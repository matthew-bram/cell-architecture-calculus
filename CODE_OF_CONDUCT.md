# Code of conduct

This is a repository about a method, and the discussions in it are about designs, models, theorems and traces. Three rules keep them that way.

1. **Argue about the artifact.** A claim is right or wrong by what the design says, what the kernel checks and what the trace shows. Say which, and point at the row, the theorem or the step. Never argue about the person who wrote it.
2. **Report what you found, not what you assume.** A rejected trace, a surviving mutant, a transcription that differed: say what happened and what you expected, with the commands to reproduce it. A finding is more useful than a verdict.
3. **Be the reviewer you would want.** Reviews here are what the method rests on. Read the whole change, say what blocks and what does not, and sign only for what you read.

Harassment, personal attacks and discrimination of any kind are not tolerated. A maintainer may remove comments, decline contributions or block participants who break these rules. Report a problem privately to the maintainers through the repository's security advisories if it concerns conduct in a public thread.
